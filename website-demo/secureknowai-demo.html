SecureKnowAI

A privacy-first local AI knowledge assistant that allows businesses to ask natural-language questions about internal documents without sending sensitive information to the cloud.

What is SecureKnowAI?

SecureKnowAI is a prototype designed to demonstrate how organisations can use AI to search and understand internal knowledge while keeping sensitive information on the local machine.

🔒 Privacy First

Documents and questions can remain within the local environment instead of being sent to an external AI service.

🤖 Natural Language

Users can ask normal business questions instead of manually searching through large documents.

⚡ Local AI

The prototype uses a locally running AI model, making it suitable for privacy-sensitive scenarios.

SecureKnowAI Test Results

The prototype was tested against 16 business questions covering employee information, revenue data, product performance and company policies. All 16 test cases passed.

1. What is Aisha Farouk's job title? Expected: Support Team Lead Result: PASS
2. What is Aisha Farouk's job title and location? Expected: Support Team Lead; Remote Result: PASS
3. Who is Aisha Farouk's manager? Expected: Robert Nguyen (E1013) Result: PASS
4. Who is the manager of employee E1002? Expected: Sofia Petrov (E1010) Result: PASS
5. What is James Okafor's job title? Expected: Software Engineer Result: PASS
6. Where does James Okafor work? Expected: Remote Result: PASS
7. What department does Maria Santos work in? Expected: Engineering Result: PASS
8. What is the total revenue for Q1? Expected: $225,224.60 Result: PASS
9. What is the total revenue for Q2? Expected: $252,017.17 Result: PASS
10. Which product generated the most revenue in Q2? Expected: 3-Season Tent — $93,746.25 Result: PASS
11. How many 3-Season Tents were sold in the West in Q2? Expected: 210 units Result: PASS
12. How many PTO days can employees carry over? Expected: 5 days Result: PASS
13. What is the home office equipment stipend? Expected: $600 Result: PASS
14. How many days per week can eligible employees work remotely? Expected: Up to 4 days Result: PASS
15. Can Aisha Farouk work remotely according to the employee directory and employee handbook? Expected: Explain Aisha's eligibility using the directory and handbook. SecureKnowAI answered that Aisha works in Customer Support and can work remotely up to 4 days per week, subject to policy conditions and manager approval. Result: PASS
16. What is Aisha Farouk's phone number? Expected: Information not available; the system should NOT invent an answer. SecureKnowAI correctly reported that the information could not be found in the uploaded documents. Result: PASS

Why It Matters

Many organisations want to benefit from generative AI but are concerned about sending confidential company information to external services. SecureKnowAI demonstrates one possible approach: keeping the AI workflow local while providing a simple question-and-answer experience for employees.
